### Information 

**Textbook:**

Custom UC Berkeley Edition, combining in a single volume parts of

-   David Lay, *Linear Algebra*, 5th ed., and
-   Nagle, Saff & Snider, *Fundamentals of differential equations and boundary value problems,* 9th ed*.* 

**Homework and quizzes:** 

Homework is due on Thursday at the beginning of class. No late homework will be accepted. Quizzes will take place every Thursday in the last twenty minutes. Quizzes will cover homework material due on Monday. It is your responsibility to make your work clear: if you are “right” but the grader did not notice, you will lose points.

The lowest two homework scores and the lowest two quiz scores will be dropped.

**Attendance:** 

I do not keep track of attendance (though you should appear for the quizzes!). It is up to you to determine how you are going to learn the material.
