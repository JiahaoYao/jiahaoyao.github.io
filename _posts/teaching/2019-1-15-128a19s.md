### Information 

**Textbook:** (BFB) Burden, Faires and Burden, *Numerical Analysis*, 10th edition, 2015.

**Homework and quizzes:** 

Homework is due every Wednesday. No late submissions, lowest score dropped.  

Quizzes will take place in the first twenty minutes of the sections. No makeups, lowest score dropped. 

**Attendance:** 

I do not keep track of attendance (though you should appear for the quizzes!). It is up to you to determine how you are going to learn the material.

**Exams** All exams and quizzes are closed-book closed-notes: Written, typed and printed materials and electronics are not permitted. No makeup exams will be given. 

**Grades** The course grade will be computed from the formula:

`0.25 * problem sets + 0.15 * quizzes + 0.25 * max( midterm , final ) + 0.35 * final`. 

 Thus the final exam score will override a lower or missing midterm score. 

**DSP accommodations:** Please tell me so that arrangements can be made.
