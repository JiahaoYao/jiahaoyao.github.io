Hello World!

Finally, I decide to use this template for my blog. I will be my technique blog, and I will write all about my research stuff. It is sure to be a fantastic thing to maintain in my life.

I am so lucky to get to know the blog coincidentally when I search the research paper about Markov Chain and Generative Adversarial Network. It seems that I fall in love with the simple style and I also admire Jiaming Song1. For sake of setting as an example of me, I heuristically use his template for the blog. And in the end, I sincerely thank him.

Also, Along the way, I am also grateful to other people who have helped me with my dream of building up my personal pages. First, there is another homepage Zi Wang’s Homepage. I really love the Font used in the word “Zi Wang’s Homepage”. Almost when I am discouraged to get the font in my new homepage, I turn to Slinuxer 2 for help. He gives me the great insight of how to use the Chrome for debugging, with which technique I succeed in getting the font name of “Zi Wang’s Homepage” and Hex color of Princeton’s Orange. Finally, Our trouble becomes how to local generate the homepage using the jekyll. Since using the Mac, there might be some problems about installing the packages, which is not easy compared with the Linux. THe Biggest problem lies in the Edition of Ruby and the installation of the nokogiri packages and I hope this blog and Official Blog can help if you have the same problems.

After all these trouble-shooting, I really appreciate all the lovely people around me and they help me get what I want. And I sincerely hope that the homepage will give me more insight and thinking about the life and research.
